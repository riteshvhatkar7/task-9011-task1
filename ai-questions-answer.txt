Answer: AI that creates new content.

Q.13. What is a prompt?
Answer: An instruction or question given to AI.

Q.14. What is a system prompt?
Answer: An instruction that guides AI behavior.

Q.15. What is an AI hallucination?
Answer: When AI gives false information as fact.

Q.16. What is a token in an LLM?
Answer: A small unit of text.

Q.17. What is a context window?
Answer: Information an AI can process at one time.

Q.18. What is inference in AI?
Answer: Using a trained model to make predictions.

Q.19. What is fine-tuning?
Answer: Further training a model for a specific task.

Q.20. What does multimodal AI mean?
Answer: AI that handles different types of data.
